The Shattered Veil Scenario Background

Part Two: The Broken Bridge

The crossing at Mireth's Ford lies in ruins — and something stirs beneath
Danger
Area Description

"The bridge has not been whole in living memory. Its broken arches jut from the black water like the ribs of some drowned leviathan. The river here moves slow and heavy, thick with silt from the marshes upstream, and the air smells of wet stone and old iron. On the far bank, the road vanishes into a stand of skeletal birches whose bare branches claw at a sky the colour of a bruise."

Mireth's Crossing was once the only fordable point on the River Oss for thirty miles in either direction. The old stone bridge — three arches wide, built in the second age — collapsed during the Veilquake fifteen years ago. The Veiled King's engineers never repaired it; the Frayed Marches were deemed unworthy of resource. What remains are two crumbled stone piers amid the current and a single cracked span on the near side wide enough for two abreast.

The debris field in the river conceals more than rubble. The Nullstone fragments that triggered the Veilquake still pulse weakly beneath the silt, and the water around the piers shimmers with a faint oily iridescence that is not natural. Local superstition warns against lingering at the crossing after dark. This superstition is correct.

Points of Interest
The Central Pier: The largest surviving structure. Cracked down the middle but stable enough to cross with care (DC 10 Acrobatics, or normal movement at half speed). Beneath the pier, a submerged opening leads to the drowned chamber below — home to something that was not there before the bridge fell.
The Debris Field: Rubble and broken masonry fill the river between the piers. Difficult terrain for any creature that enters the water. A DC 14 Investigation check reveals worked stone — carved blocks, not natural rock — suggesting the bridge's collapse exposed something older.
The Warning Stones: Two standing stones on the near bank, carved in a script older than the Veiled King's reign. They are Nullstone wards — still partially active. Any Veil-constructed creature that approaches within 10 feet must make a DC 13 Wisdom save or be turned for 1 round.
The Traveller's Bundle: A rotten leather pack wedged beneath a fallen arch. Inside: a waterlogged journal (the last ten entries are illegible), a pouch containing 14 sp and 3 gp, and an iron key stamped with a symbol the PCs will recognise from Emberford's shrine. Whoever left this bundle did not leave alive.

The Broken Bridge works best as a transitional encounter — tense but not necessarily combat-focused. The real threat beneath the pier should be foreshadowed (scrapes on the stone, objects pulled under, the water's wrongness) before it emerges. If the party has been marked by the Nullstone before, the creature below recognises them and calls their name in a voice like wet stone grinding together.

"Old Mireth built that bridge with her own hands. They say she poured her blood into the mortar so it would hold forever. It held for three hundred years. Then the Veil took what was owed."
