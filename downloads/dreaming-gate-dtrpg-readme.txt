THE DREAMING GATE
A D&D 5e Mystery-Horror Adventure for Levels 1–3

The village of Drearym is dying. Crops fail, livestock sicken, and nightmares
plague every soul. The source: a rift between worlds, hidden in the ruins beneath
the old temple. The party must uncover the truth, navigate faction politics, and
decide the fate of a gateway that should never have been opened.

This is the first adventure in The Hollow Dominion series — a mystery-horror
campaign that begins at Level 1 and builds across multiple books. Designed for
Game Masters who want atmosphere, investigation, and moral weight without the
prep burden.

WHAT'S INSIDE:
• 3-part mystery-horror adventure for Levels 1–3
• 3–4 sessions with a cliffhanger into Book 2
• Detailed village of Drearym with key NPCs
• Investigation paths with multiple solutions
• Rift-between-worlds horror theme with faction politics
• NPC stat blocks, maps, and handouts included
• Three possible endings that affect Book 2
• Designer's Notes and About the Author

ADVENTURE DETAILS:
— Levels: 1–3
— Players: 3–5
— Sessions: 3–4 (3–4 hours each)
— Theme: Mystery / Horror / Investigation
— Series: The Hollow Dominion (Book 1)
— Author: Helm Evans
— Publisher: Tuco Enterprises

This product contains adult themes including nightmares, corruption, and moral
ambiguity. Player discretion advised.
