Loot, Encounters, NPCs, Magic Items & Locations — For D&D 5e and Other Fantasy RPGs
A complete set of random tables for game masters. Roll when you need treasure, encounters, NPCs, or locations — during prep or in the middle of a session. All tables use a d20 (or d10/d6 where noted) and are designed for D&D 5e but work in any fantasy RPG.
