d20 Magic & Miscast Effects

Roll when a spell goes wrong, wild magic surges, or the Veil interferes with arcane power
When to Roll
Roll on the Miscast Table when a spellcaster rolls a natural 1 on a spell attack, fails a spellcasting check by 5 or more, or when the Veil is thin (DM's discretion). Roll on the Wild Magic Table for sorcerers, wild magic zones, or when someone taps into raw Veil energy.

Miscast effects should feel dangerous but not campaign-ending. The worst results (18–20) are dramatic and memorable, not party-killers. Wild magic surges should lean fun and chaotic — they're the moments players talk about later. If a result doesn't fit the scene, roll again or pick something that does.
