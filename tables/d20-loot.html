The Shattered Veil Random Tables — Loot

d20 Loot & Treasure

Roll when the party searches a body, opens a chest, or explores a ruin
How This Works
Roll a d20 whenever the party finds loot. For mundane treasure, roll on the Common Finds table (1–12). For something magical or unusual, roll on the Rare & Remarkable table (13–20). The DM can bias the roll based on context — a dead bandit gets +0, a dragon's hoard gets +5.
