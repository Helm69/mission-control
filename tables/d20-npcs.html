d20 NPC Quirks & Secrets

Roll when you need a memorable character in seconds
How to Use
Roll once for a Quirk (left table) and once for a Secret (right table) to instantly create a fully-formed NPC. Combine any quirk with any secret — the results should surprise you. For extra depth, roll a second quirk on a 15+.
☺ NPC Quirks (d20)
d20Quirk
1Compulsive Counter
Counts everything — steps, coins, words. Taps their fingers constantly.
2Whispers to Themselves
Full conversations with an invisible companion. The companion has opinions.
3Obsessive Polisher
Cleans and polishes one item obsessively — a coin, a ring, a dagger. It's never clean enough.
4Terrible Liar
Blushes, stammers, and over-explains. Everyone knows they're lying. They don't know everyone knows.
5Collector of Teeth
Keeps a pouch of teeth — animal, human, monster. Offers them as gifts.
6Speaks in Third Person
Refers to themselves by name. Insists it's "more professional."
7Afraid of Birds
Panics at the sight or sound of birds. Won't go near a coop or aviary.
8Hums Battle Hymns
Constantly humming old war songs. Doesn't realise they're doing it. The songs are banned.
9Overly Formal
Uses titles and courtly language even in a tavern. Calls everyone "my lord" or "my lady."
10Tells the Same Story
Has one story they tell at every opportunity. It gets longer each time.
11Refuses to Sit
Always stands, even when offered a chair. Claims sitting "slows the blood."
12Paints Their Nails
With whatever is available — berry juice, soot, blood. Different colour each day.
13Allergic to Magic
Sneezes, itches, or breaks out in hives near spellcasters. Genuinely physiological.
14Carries a Lucky Stone
A specific, unremarkable stone. Will panic if separated from it. Has a name for it.
15Eats with Their Hands
Even soup. Especially soup. Refuses cutlery on principle.
16Sleepwalker
Has been found in strange places. Once woke up on the roof. Doesn't remember.
17Veil-Touched Eyes
Their eyes occasionally flash silver. They claim it's a trick of the light. It isn't.
18Writes Everything Down
Carries a journal and writes in it constantly. Will read passages aloud unprompted.
19Superstitious to a Fault
Won't walk under ladders, salt thresholds, or speak certain words. Has a ritual for everything.
20Perfect Memory
Remembers every conversation, every face, every slight. Brings up things you said three years ago.
✦ NPC Secrets (d20)
d20Secret
1Fugitive Identity
They're not who they say they are. They're running from a crime — real or perceived — committed years ago.
2Veil Addict
They've been using Veil-touched substances. It's changing them. They need the next fix.
3Secret Heir
They're the illegitimate child of someone important. They don't know it yet. Someone else does.
4Double Agent
They're feeding information to an enemy faction. They believe they're doing the right thing.
5Cursed Bloodline
Something in their family's past invoked a curse. It's waking up. They're the last of the line.
6Stolen Power
Their magical ability isn't natural — they took it from someone. That someone is looking for it back.
7Witness to Atrocity
They saw something they shouldn't have — a massacre, a ritual, a betrayal. They've never told anyone.
8Owes a Debt to the Veil
They made a bargain with something from beyond. The payment is coming due.
9Plague Carrier
They're infected with something — magical or mundane — and don't know it. They're contagious.
10Former Enemy
They used to serve the antagonist. They defected. Their old comrades don't know they're alive.
11Hidden Treasure Map
They have — or are — a map to something valuable. They may not realise what they're carrying.
12Prophesied
An old prophecy mentions them by description. They're either the saviour or the destroyer.
13Possessed Object
Something they carry — a ring, a book, a weapon — contains a trapped spirit. It's waking up.
14Blackmailed
Someone has leverage over them. They're being forced to act against their own interests.
15Dead Ringer
They look exactly like someone famous — or infamous. Mistaken identity is a constant problem.
16Sleeper Agent
They've been magically conditioned to act on a trigger phrase. They don't know it exists.
17Guardian of a Seal
They're the latest in a line of guardians protecting a Veil seal. They inherited the duty. The seal is weakening.
18Murderer
They killed someone. It might have been justified. It might not have been. They live with it every day.
19Changeling
They were swapped at birth — fae, Veil-creature, or something else. Their "parents" may or may not know.
20The Key
They are literally a key — their blood, their life force, or their death is required to open something that should stay closed.
The best NPCs are contradictions. A compulsive counter who's also a terrible liar. A fugitive who paints their nails. A secret heir who's afraid of birds. Roll, combine, and let the unexpected pairings create characters your players will remember. And always — always — let the players discover the secret through play, not exposition.
