The Shattered Veil Random Tables — Encounters

d20 Random Encounters

Roll when the party travels, camps, or explores the unknown
When to Roll
Roll once per travel day and once per night watch. On a roll of 18–20, the encounter is significant — it should advance a plot thread or reveal something new about the world. On 1–5, it's atmospheric — colour, tension, or a minor obstacle.

3 soldiers in mismatched armour, deserters from the Veiled King's army. They're hungry and desperate but not hostile — unless provoked.		SocialLow

6Nullstone Quarry

7The Hanged Man

8Fog of Lost Things
A thick fog rolls in. For 1d4 hours, the party can only see 30 feet. Lost items from previous travellers appear in the mist — a boot, a sword, a child's doll.		WeirdNone
9Bandit Ambush
4 bandits in hiding. They demand half the party's coin. A DC 15 Intimidation or Persuasion check avoids combat.		Combat SocialLow
10The Oracle's Tent
A small, impossible tent sits by the road. Inside: an old woman who answers one question truthfully — then vanishes. The tent is gone when the party looks back.		WeirdNone
11Collapsed Bridge
The path forward is blocked. A DC 14 Athletics check to climb, or 2d6 hours to find a way around. While delayed, roll again on this table.		ExploreNone
12Veil-Touched Beast
A deer with too many eyes. It watches the party pass. If attacked, it doesn't fight — it screams, and every creature within a mile hears it.		WeirdLow
13The Refugee Train
A dozen families fleeing the northern war. They have nothing to trade but stories — and one of them is carrying a sealed letter meant for someone in the party.		SocialNone
14Spectral Re-enactment
The party witnesses a ghostly battle from the Veilquake — soldiers fighting shadows, a bridge collapsing. It repeats every 10 minutes. A DC 16 Arcana check lets a character interact with it.		WeirdNone
15The Toll-Keeper
A massive figure in rusted armour blocks the road. It demands a "toll of memory" — one character must surrender a cherished memory (disadvantage on one skill check type for 24 hours) to pass. Or fight.		Combat SocialHigh
16Wounded Knight
A knight of the Veiled King, badly injured, begs the party to deliver a message to the capital. She dies within the hour regardless — but the message is genuine and important.		SocialNone
17The Fungal Bloom
A field of enormous mushrooms, some 10 feet tall. Beautiful. Deeply wrong. Spores cause hallucinations (DC 14 Con save or be poisoned for 1 hour). Something large moves among them.		Explore CombatMedium
18The Veil Rift
A tear in reality, 5 feet wide, crackling with energy. Something is trying to come through. The party has 3 rounds to close it (Arcana DC 15) or fight what emerges.		Combat WeirdHigh
19The Traitor's Camp
A camp of enemy soldiers — the same ones the party has been tracking. They're asleep. A perfect ambush opportunity. Or a chance to listen.		Combat ExploreHigh
20The Shattered One
A figure made of broken mirror-glass and old armour. It speaks in the voice of someone the party knows. It knows things it shouldn't. It offers a bargain — a piece of information for a piece of the party's past.		Weird Social???
Not every encounter needs to be a fight. The best random tables create moments — a weeping stone, a singing hollow, a merchant with coloured water. Use the low-threat entries to build atmosphere and the high-threat ones to escalate tension. And always, always let the players' choices matter more than the dice.
