The Shattered Veil Random Tables — World Events

d20 World Events & Rumours

Roll at the start of each in-game week or when the party enters a new settlement
How This Works
Roll on the World Events table for things happening now — events the party can witness, interact with, or be affected by. Roll on the Rumours & Whispers table for things people are talking about — some true, some false, all potentially useful. Roll 1d4 rumours per settlement visited.
☀ World Events (d20)
d20EventTag
1The Silver Drought
No rain for 40 days. Wells are low. Tensions between farming villages are rising. Someone's been poisoning water stores.		Natural Political
2Veilquake Aftershock
A minor tremor — but it opens a new fissure. Something crawls out. The local lord has put a bounty on "fissure creatures."		Veil
3The Conscription
The Veiled King's army is pressing men into service. Villages are hiding their young. Deserters are everywhere.		War Political
4Plague of Shadows
A village reports that people's shadows are moving independently. Three people have gone missing. The local priest is baffled.		Veil Natural
5The Merchant Prince Arrives
A wealthy trader has set up in the market town. Prices are dropping — he's buying everything. No one knows where his wealth comes from.		Economy
6Festival of the Veil
An annual celebration where the boundary between worlds is honoured. This year, the rituals feel... different. More real.		Veil
7The Bridge Collapse
A major trade bridge has fallen. Supply lines are disrupted. Bandits are exploiting the detour routes.		Natural Economy
8Heretic's Trial
A woman claims the Veil is not a barrier but a wound. She's been arrested for heresy. A crowd is gathering.		Political
9The Beast of the Moors
Livestock are being killed — drained of blood, not eaten. A hunting party went out. Only one returned, and he won't speak.		Natural
10Nullstone Rush
A vein of Nullstone has been discovered. Miners, wizards, and criminals are all converging. The local economy is booming — and so is the violence.		Economy Veil
11The Silent Army
A column of soldiers marches through at night. They don't stop, don't speak, don't respond. By morning, they're gone. Tracks lead to the Veil fissure.		War Veil
12Harvest Failure
The crops are wrong. Grain grows black. Fruit tastes of ash. The druids say the land is grieving.		Natural
13The Assassination
A regional governor has been killed. The killer left a Veil-touched weapon at the scene. Fingers point in every direction.		Political War
14Refugee Crisis
Hundreds of displaced people arrive from the northern war zone. The town can't feed them. Tensions are rising between locals and refugees.		War Political
15The Awakening
An ancient statue in the town square has moved. Its eyes now face the Veil fissure. It wasn't facing that direction yesterday.		Veil
16Trade Embargo
The Veiled King has banned trade with the southern provinces. Smuggling is now the only option. Prices have tripled.		Political Economy
17The Light in the Sky
A second sun appeared at dawn. It lasted for one hour, then vanished. Scholars are panicking. The common folk are praying.		Veil Natural
18Uprising
Peasants have seized a garrison. They're demanding an end to conscription and the Veil tithe. The King's response will be swift.		War Political
19The Vanishing
An entire hamlet — 30 people — has disappeared overnight. The buildings remain. The food is still warm. No tracks lead away.		Veil
20The Veil Thins
Every fissure in the region pulses simultaneously. For one hour, the boundary between worlds is transparent. Everyone sees what's on the other side.		Veil
👂 Rumours & Whispers (d20)
d20RumourVeracity
1"The Veiled King is dead."
Someone saw the royal seal being moved at night. The court denies everything.		True
2"There's a door in the old mine that leads to another world."
A miner swears he walked through and came out fifty miles away.		True
3"The Veil was created deliberately — to keep something in."
An old scholar's theory. She was laughed out of the academy.		True
4"A dragon has been seen over the northern peaks."
Could be a dragon. Could be a Veil-stalker. Could be a very large bird.		Uncertain
5"The Nullstone can cure any disease."
It can't. But people are paying fortunes for it anyway.		False
6"The party is being watched."
By whom? The rumour doesn't say. But someone's been asking questions.		True
7"A treasure ship sank in the marsh with a fortune in gold."
It did. The marsh is haunted. Most who go looking don't come back.		True
8"The Veil is a living thing."
It breathes. It hungers. And it's getting thinner.		Uncertain
9"A child was born with silver eyes. The midwife was struck blind."
The child exists. The blindness is disputed.		Uncertain
10"The Veiled King's army is deserting en masse."
Some are. Not most. But the rumour is spreading faster than the truth.		Partly True
11"There's a weapon that can kill anything from the Veil."
There is. It's been lost for centuries. Someone is looking for it.		True
12"The stars are wrong."
Astronomers confirm: three stars have moved. No one knows what it means.		True
13"A noble is secretly funding the resistance."
Which noble? Depends on who you ask. Everyone accuses someone different.		Uncertain
14"The dead are restless."
Gravediggers report empty coffins. Families say they've heard knocking from below.		True
15"A map to the Veil's heart has been found."
It's a fragment. It's incomplete. It's already caused two murders.		True
16"The Veil chose the party."
A fortune teller said this. She's been right before.		Uncertain
17"Wine from the southern vineyards causes prophetic dreams."
It does. The merchants have been adding something.		True
18"A second Veil is forming."
Scholars have detected anomalies. If true, the implications are catastrophic.		Uncertain
19"The party's patron has a hidden agenda."
Of course they do. The question is: what?		True
20"The world is a dream, and something is waking up."
A mad prophet screams this in the market square. No one listens. Yet.		???
Rumours are the lifeblood of a living world. Not every rumour should be true — some are lies, some are misunderstandings, some are half-truths. Let the players investigate, debate, and choose what to believe. The best rumours are the ones that make players say "wait, what if that's real?" And remember: a rumour the players ignore is a plot thread you can pull later.
